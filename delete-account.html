Delete your Alma account

You can do this here, without the app. It happens when you confirm it — not a request you send and wait on.

Have the app? It is quicker there: open Alma, then Me → Delete everything. That also clears the record from the phone itself, which this page cannot reach.

What is deleted

  • Your account and your email address
  • The copy of your record, if you kept one, and your scan pictures with it
  • Your messages in the rooms, your private messages, your friendships and your reports
  • Where another mother quoted you in a reply, the quote. Her reply stays: those words are hers.

A room you made is not deleted for everyone in it — it passes to another of its admins or members. Photos you sent in the last 30 days are deleted from the server automatically within that time.

Your record on your phone is not touched by this page. To remove it too, remove the Alma app from the phone.

1. Your email address

The one you sign in to Alma with. We will send a six-digit code to it, so that only the person who can open that email can delete the account.

2. The code from your email

If an Alma account uses that address, a code is on its way. It can take a minute, and may be in spam.

3. Delete it

This deletes the account and everything listed above. It cannot be undone. A new account can be made later, but it will start empty.

Your account has been deleted

Everything listed above has gone from Alma's server. If Alma is still on a phone, remove the app from it to remove the record there as well.

If you ever want Alma again, you are welcome back.
